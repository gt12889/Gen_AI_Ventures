# Welcome to my GenAI Business Senior Capstone project BPS 4395.501.

This project was completed in collaboration with a partner company, where I helped turn their business idea into a working web application. The goal was to take real business requirements, design a solution from scratch, and build a functional prototype that the company can continue developing after the course.
This capstone was built for Amit, our sponsor, who shared their business idea and worked with me throughout the process. They provided feedback on the design, helped define the main features, and ensured the final product aligned with what they needed.

This project is built with .

- Vite
- TypeScript
- React
- shadcn-ui
- Tailwind CSS


Project Summary

Purpose
The goal of this project was to help our partner company build an internal product for competitive market analysis. The tool is intended for proprietary use and designed to support better decision-making in fast-moving markets.

Problem We Addressed

The company did not have a product in place for market or competitor analysis

There was limited understanding of the current AI-driven analysis landscape

No clear structure existed for turning raw market data into actionable insights

Because of this, the business lacked a reliable way to evaluate competitors, trends, and opportunities.
